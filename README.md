Entry for the make.fun bounty [Two-Sentence Horror](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253)
(slug `u-suroto-two-sentence-horror-acea9253`), by @expylar.

The program writes one horror story of exactly two sentences, at most
400 characters, to `/output/entry.txt`. It validates its own gates at
runtime (exactly two sentences, 1–400 characters, no trailing space,
ends with sentence-final punctuation) and writes the same bytes on
every run, with no network access.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. The story text lives in `main.py`.

The story (214 characters, exactly two sentences):

> My daughter sleeps with her night light on because she says the dark
> has hands. Tonight I unplugged it to prove her wrong, and in the dark
> something small took my hand and whispered, 'Thank you for turning
> it off.'

Why this story: the first sentence sets up a parent who does not
believe the child; the second sentence makes the parent's own
skepticism the trigger — unplugging the light is what lets the dark
in, and the thing in the dark thanks him for it. The horror is
implied (a small hand, a whisper), never explained.
